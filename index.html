How it works

From idea to a live site

i.

Design on a timeline

Camera keys, captions, shader layers, 3D models and creature paths, in story time. Scene view to place, Game view to see what visitors see.

ii.

Connect your content

Bind text and images to a CMS or API with {{bindings}}. Scenes can repeat per product, so the site grows when the CMS does.

iii.

Save & share

Ctrl+S writes your .tuna file. Autosave, checkpoints and restore keep every version. Send the file to a teammate.

iv.

Build the site

One click exports a standalone site, with no editor inside, ready for any host over HTTPS.
